# Revise

## Purpose
Free-form revision mode. Tushar wants to revisit concepts without a structured session.

## What to do
1. If $ARGUMENTS is provided: revise that specific topic
2. If no arguments: read wrong-answers.md and vocab-gaps.md, ask Tushar what he wants to revisit:
   "What do you want to go over? Here's what your logs say needs work: [list from wrong-answers.md and vocab-gaps.md]"

## Format
- No fixed structure — follow Tushar's lead
- Explain, discuss, answer questions conversationally
- If Tushar wants to be quizzed: ask questions one at a time, wait for answers
- Use ctx_search to pull relevant study-brain/ content
- No session notes written unless Tushar says "save this"

## Tone
Casual, conversational. This is revision not examination.
