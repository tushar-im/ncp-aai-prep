### 2026-05-08 — Agent Architecture and Design

**Term**: Anchoring bias
**Means**: When an evaluator agent sees prior agents' reasoning chains, it anchors to their conclusions and scores leniently instead of evaluating independently
**Don't confuse with**: confirmation bias (different concept, not exam-relevant here)

**Term**: Blackboard pattern
**Means**: Multi-agent pattern where agents communicate through a shared passive data store — no direct agent-to-agent communication
**Don't confuse with**: Orchestrator-worker (centralized planner dispatching tasks) or pipeline+feedback (PACE)
